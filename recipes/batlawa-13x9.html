Batlawa (13×9 Pyrex Baking Dish)

Lebanese baklava scaled for a 13×9 Pyrex dish — phyllo #4, walnuts, clarified butter & orange blossom syrup

Pan
13×9″ Pyrex
Oven
300°F
Bake
1¼–1½ hrs
Rack
Lower (not lowest)
Servings
10+
Category
Lebanese · Dessert

Ingredients

Phyllo Dough

  • 1 lb package phyllo dough, #4 (thinnest sheets)
    Recommended: The Fillo Factory #4 (14″ × 18″)

Clarified Butter

  • 1½ lbs butter (salted or unsalted) — clarified

Walnut Filling

  • 1½ lbs walnuts, ground coarse
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ¼ tsp orange blossom water (Mazaher)

Syrup (make first)

  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup water
  • ⅞ Tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1 Tablespoon orange blossom water (Mazaher) — added after cooling

Directions

Critical: The syrup must be completely cold before pouring over hot batlawa. Make the syrup the night before or early in the day.

Make the syrup first — it must cool completely

  1. Stir 2 cups granulated sugar and ¾ cup water in a saucepan over medium-high heat until the mixture is clear and comes to a boil.

  2. Add ⅞ Tablespoon fresh lemon juice. Stir until settled, then simmer on low for 10–15 minutes. Remove from heat to a back burner.

    The syrup will not seem thick on the stove. It thickens as it cools off the heat.

    Once completely cool, stir in 1 Tablespoon of orange blossom water. Do not refrigerate.

Assemble the batlawa

Clarified butter: Melt on low heat. Let white milk solids settle to the bottom. Skim any foam from the top. Use only the clear golden liquid.
Phyllo tip: Bring frozen phyllo to room temperature before opening. Keep covered with a damp towel until ready to use.
  1. Preheat oven to 300°F.
  2. Using 1.5 pounds of walnuts, working 1 cup at a time, pulse walnuts in a Cuisinart for up to 14 pulses to coarse, not fine. Mix with 1 cup granulated sugar and ¼ tsp orange blossom water. Set aside.
  3. Butter the 13×9″ Pyrex dish generously with clarified butter, making sure the sides are well-covered.
  4. Remove phyllo from package. Cut the entire stack in half to fit the dish. Place all layers from one half on the bottom of the pan as the base.
  5. Spread the walnut filling evenly over the bottom phyllo layers. Sprinkle clarified butter over the walnut mixture.
  6. Place the remaining phyllo layers on top of the walnuts. Tuck the edges of the phyllo into the sides of the Pyrex dish.
  7. Using a ruler, cut batlawa into diamonds on the diagonal — cut along each side of the ruler for even lines. Reverse the pan and cut diagonally in the other direction to form the diamond pattern.
  8. After cutting, pour all of the remaining clarified butter evenly over the top of the phyllo.
  9. Bake on the lower rack (not the lowest) at 300°F for 1¼ to 1½ hours, until light golden brown. To check doneness, gently lift a top layer — the dough should be crispy all the way down to the nuts.
  10. Remove from oven. Set on a rack and let rest 5 minutes. While batlawa is still hot, pour the cold syrup evenly over the top. You will hear it sizzle.
Storing: Batlawa keeps at room temperature for up to 2 weeks, covered loosely. Do not refrigerate — it will lose its crispness.
